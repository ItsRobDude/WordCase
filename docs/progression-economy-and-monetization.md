# WordCase Progression, Economy, and Monetization

This document defines official WordCase progression, reward, and monetization rules.

Its purpose is to keep WordCase fair, calm, and identity-driven while preventing economy and monetization systems from deforming core puzzle trust.

This is a source-of-truth product document.
When implementation details or UI concepts disagree with this document, this document should be treated as the intended progression/economy rulebook until intentionally updated.

---

## 1. Core Principles

WordCase progression and monetization should:
- protect fairness first
- preserve canonical puzzle dignity
- reward consistency and identity, not spending pressure
- stay lightweight and readable
- remain secondary to daily and weekly puzzle truth

WordCase should not monetize frustration, confusion, or basic accessibility.

---

## 2. V1 Economy Scope

### 2.1 V1 currencies
WordCase uses no currencies in v1.

V1 does not include:
- coins
- gems
- tickets
- premium currency
- XP bars
- keys
- booster inventory
- chest systems

### 2.2 V1 reward shape beyond streak/evidence/history
In v1, solve rewards beyond streak/evidence/history are:
- clean result moment
- attempts-used summary
- assisted or unassisted classification
- weekly board movement
- solved-case stat growth

Later lightweight identity rewards may include:
- dossier stamps
- case badges
- weekly case cards
- archive growth

No loot or randomized reward systems are allowed.

---

## 3. Weekly Completion Rewards

Weekly completion rewards are:
- resolved weekly board state
- permanent weekly completion record
- stronger case-closed presentation beat
- visible weekly-completion stat increase

Later identity/collection rewards may include:
- dossier seals
- weekly case cards
- weekly file entries

Optional later unlocks may include:
- curated bonus practice file
- archive entry
- subtle profile marker

No currency-style weekly rewards are allowed.

---

## 4. Hint and Economy Relationship

Canonical hint rules:
- canonical Daily Case hints are always free-per-case
- canonical Weekly Resolution hints are always free-per-case
- no paid extra canonical hints
- no rewarded-ad extra canonical hints
- no buying more chances

Early WordCase rule:
- all hints remain free-per-case everywhere in early WordCase

Future guardrail:
- if archive/practice convenience assists are ever monetized later, they must remain clearly non-canonical and should be deferred aggressively

---

## 5. Unlocks and Identity Surfaces

### 5.1 Profile unlock direction
Profile progression focuses on identity/history signals such as:
- streak
- solves
- weekly completions
- assisted/unassisted stats
- solved-case history
- later badges, seals, and theme ownership

### 5.2 Archive unlock direction
Archive progression focuses on playable/history surfaces such as:
- starter replay
- practice files
- past dailies
- weekly history later
- premium archive packs later

### 5.3 Collections unlock direction
Collections focus on thematic identity/history such as:
- weekly case cards
- dossier seals or stamps
- solved file entries
- themed sets later

Unlocks should stay light, thematic, and identity/history-based.

---

## 6. Monetization Scope by Milestone

Monetization rollout rules:
- no player-facing monetization in milestone 2
- no player-facing monetization in milestone 3
- no player-facing monetization in milestone 4
- milestone 4 may include invisible entitlement groundwork only if needed
- first real player-facing monetization belongs in milestone 6

---

## 7. Preferred First Monetization Items Later

When player-facing monetization begins later, preferred first items are:
- one-time Ad-Free or Supporter purchase
- cosmetic theme packs
- premium archive or special investigation packs
- campaign-style DLC or themed mission packs

These should remain secondary to puzzle fairness and daily-first clarity.

---

## 8. Explicitly Forbidden Monetization

WordCase must not monetize any of the following:
- extra canonical daily attempts
- extra canonical weekly attempts
- extra canonical daily hints
- extra canonical weekly hints
- buying streak repair
- buying weekly evidence
- buying weekly unlock thresholds
- buying answer reveals for live canonical play
- paying to convert a fail into a solve
- forced ads during active solving
- forced ads before result clarity
- accessibility settings
- large text support
- reduced motion support
- sound/haptics toggles
- basic save/restore dignity
- basic earned profile/history visibility

---

## 9. Strongly Discouraged Models

The following are strongly discouraged:
- subscriptions
- battle passes
- premium currencies
- push-pressure monetization
- monetizing frustrations created by the game itself

---

## 10. Summary Rule

WordCase progression should feel like case-solving identity growth, not currency treadmill management.

When in doubt, prefer:
- fairness
- clarity
- dignity
- thematic identity rewards

over monetization pressure, manipulative systems, or spend-to-repair mechanics.
